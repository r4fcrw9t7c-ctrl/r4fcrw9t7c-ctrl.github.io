Realm Certified.

STANDARDS FILE · REALM HQ · EST DAY 5

Realm Certified is the highest possible rating. It is issued by Realm HQ. Realm HQ is not registered anywhere. The rating is therefore meaningless and completely binding.

What certification means

The item, person, or transmission has passed through the Realm and survived. That is the entire test. Everything that leaves HQ is Certified before it leaves. Everything else is either in the Boneyard or under review.

  • LEVEL 04Registrar. Signs certificates. Also drinks nuttar water.

How to get certified

Complete the six-question clearance assessment. Upload a photo. Realm HQ issues a certificate. Screenshot works if download fails. The Realm does not discriminate against low-tech operatives.
